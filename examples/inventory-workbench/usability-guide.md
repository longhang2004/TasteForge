# Pending user check

Status: **not performed**. No representative warehouse users or human designers have approved this example. Agent reviews and script results belong in the design review, not in this record as user evidence.

Recruit people who actually do, or are likely to do, stock/replenishment work. For an initial qualitative round, three to five participants is a proposed practical starting point, not a statistical sample or an assurance of issue coverage. Explain that this is fictional data and no actual order can be placed. Obtain their agreement before recording or collecting notes.

Give the goal without naming the controls or showing the expected path:

1. “Find the linen tablecloth and explain what the available, incoming and shortage quantities mean for your decision.”
2. “Record a decision to replenish 8 pieces. Check what this action will do before committing.”
3. Let the simulated error happen. “Continue in the way that seems appropriate.”
4. “Return to your stock search and inspect another short item.”

Ask participants to think aloud. Observe interpretation, pauses, unintended actions, whether they recognize the no-purchase-order consequence, recovery and lost list context. Note when the moderator helped. A shorter page or a higher preference rating alone does not establish successful use.

Use the same tasks/data to compare candidates. Counterbalance order when the same person tries both; account for learning. Keep time, errors, assistance and subjective preference separate. Do not pool this round with the earlier AI-review ratings.

Record only actual observations, using anonymous participant IDs and the consented data scope:

| Participant/context | Candidate/order/viewport | Task | Observation/quote | Error or assistance | Change to test next |
| ------------------- | ------------------------ | ---- | ----------------- | ------------------- | ------------------- |

After a round, summarize repeated problems, individual exceptions and uncertainty. Update review status only when the actual review occurred, retaining the date, scope and reviewer/participant context.

Method reference, documentation inspected 2026-11-01: [GOV.UK moderated usability testing](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/using-moderated-usability-testing). It supports task-based observation and using actual/likely users; it does not validate this interface.
