## Observations and tradeoffs

**Operational:** both failure frames expose the selected long Vietnamese item name, available/reorder/shortage/incoming ledger, retained quantity 8, explicit local-only consequence and recoverable error. The source keeps query/filter controls stable, per-item drafts, keyboard entry, edit focus, error/success focus and return-trigger focus. Adapted overview/detail removes the intervening list while deciding at narrow widths and offers simultaneous comparison wide. The natural 320 failure viewport ends inside the error area: Retry is below it and requires scrolling, in both conditions. That is a visible recovery travel cost, not proof that retry is unreachable. No comparative narrow-screen win is established by these near-identical failure frames; the meaningful difference concerns the overview/detail relationship.

**Commerce:** the conventional wide product page is competent: fit specifications, choices and complete total appear in its first viewport. The adapted object sheet groups the schematic with specifications and conditions beside the purchase column, but its larger title region moves specifications lower. In the inspected adapted entry, material/dimensions extend below the first wide viewport; the adapted arrangement is therefore not an unconditional improvement. At 320 the bounded illustration/caption fit without horizontal clipping. Both natural failure frames show Sand × 2, USD 84 subtotal + USD 6 shipping = USD 90, a retained-choice error and visible Retry. Familiar select/quantity controls and the explicit schematic disclaimer support the product; the same earthy image/type direction is coherent in both conditions. Claiming a product-specificity win from the adapted arrangement alone would exceed this evidence.

**Editorial:** the adapted wide entry visibly places the rear-rail schematic beside the observation it illustrates. Source and narrow resume show that captions remain attached to figures, and record notes follow the relevant section rather than living only at the article end. The resumed 320 frame has a clear focused Compare heading, complete prose, saved-place control, figure and caption. Its record note lies below the viewport: structural association does not mean simultaneous visibility. The conventional centered prose remains plausible for uninterrupted reading; the evidence spread adds interruption and density. Both use the same paper, serif prose, numbered schematic marks and explicit uncertainty. The shared header occupies substantial initial narrow height, with contents links visible before the first section; shortening it is a future product choice rather than a demonstrated functional blocker.

**Collection:** the conventional wide grid exposes all four images/title/type/year in the first viewport and is well suited to visual discovery. The adapted index exposes material and dimensions before detail, making meaningful object differences inspectable in the results; it shows about two records in the same initial wide viewport. It trades image overview density for comparison facts. In the natural 320 return frame, `ink`, Print and the long FI-03 trigger remain in view with matching result count; lower metadata continues below. Source retains the query/type/result order and restores trigger focus; reset restores all records without rebuilding filter inputs. Archive numbering, quiet rules and original object colors are coherent, but that is an authored direction, not evidence of uniqueness among real products.

## Findings and acceptance limits

No case-module functional, factual or rendered-width blocker was identified in this bounded independent implementation review. The shared fixture controls claims; source review found no added testimonials, guarantees, stock promises, real order semantics or historical attribution. Conventional conditions retain complete detail facts and supported actions and were not visibly sabotaged to create an adaptation win.

The completed [pass-03 report](evidence/pass-03/report.json), inspected after the image review, records 144 assertions and 144 states across both conditions at 320/390/1440, with **28 `target-size` findings in the shared study navigation**. Exact affected targets are `#alternate` (about 118 × 15px) and `a[href="index.html"]` (about 62 × 15px); insufficient target size/neighbor spacing is the reported cause. The focused skip link also visibly crowds the shell navigation in entry images. This is an actual shared-shell accessibility blocker for an accepted demonstration until corrected and rechecked, rather than evidence against one condition. No module-specific node was reported for that finding. `color-contrast` remains among incomplete checks and needs the stated limits preserved. The implementation owner was notified; this reviewer made no UI edits.

Natural recovery travel, first-viewport density and reading interruption are retained as tradeoffs. They should inform conditional routing: use overview/detail for focused work, group purchase conditions near choices when relevant, attach artifacts to claims when evidence tracing matters, and expose catalog differences when comparison outweighs image-led browsing. They are not four mandatory layout presets.

Keyboard/focus behavior is supported by explicit source paths and the separately recorded automated traces; this review did not perform an assistive-technology session. A later corrected pass must supply final integrated acceptance, exact source hashes and incomplete-check scope while preserving pass-03's raw findings. Passing automation does not establish comprehension, human usability, accessibility conformance or product uniqueness. Fresh held-out evaluation remains a different evidence layer.
