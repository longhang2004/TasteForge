## Operational work — Northline Stockroom

- **Audience/task:** an operator locates a shortage and records a local replenishment decision. Reuse the maintained [inventory fixture](../inventory-workbench/fixture.json) unchanged in substance: LIN-115 has 2 available, reorder point 10, shortage 8, incoming 4, location B-04. Incoming does not remove the shortage. No live warehouse, supplier action or order.
- **Shared behavior:** search titles/SKUs, shortage filter, select item, whole quantity 1–50, review, edit, first-save recoverable failure, retry/success, return retaining query/filter/quantity/selection and useful focus. Include no-results and invalid quantity. The original maintained implementation and frozen evidence stay separate.
- **Conventional:** full-width stock comparison followed by an ordinary selected-item decision form. Competent table/list, readable labels, complete evidence at review.
- **Adaptation:** selected evidence/decision beside comparison at wide widths; focused detail replaces the overview at narrow widths, with a named return. The relationship changes from a continuous worksheet to overview/detail. Benefit hypothesis: less travel/repeated scanning while deciding; tradeoff: narrow detail loses simultaneous row comparison. Conventional may be better for continuous multi-row scanning.
- **Art direction:** warehouse ledger rules and tabular quantities; restrained green selection/action on warm neutral surfaces, retaining system fonts and long Vietnamese names. These serve stock comparison rather than marketing.
- **Checks:** query `LIN`, shortage filter, select LIN-115 by keyboard, invalid `0`/`1.5`, edit quantity 8, error/retry, retained return and trigger focus at 320/390/1440. Capture natural decision/error/return viewport and focus before optional full-page views. Compare with the maintained task contract; no new forecasting behavior.

## Commerce decision — Kiln Notes

- **Audience/task:** choose a pouring cup glaze/quantity with honest fit, care and complete purchase conditions. New fixture: stoneware, 280 ml, 9 × 8 cm, USD 42; Sand stock 4, Iron 2, Ash 0; flat USD 6 shipping; unused returns within 14 days with buyer-paid return postage; hand-wash, no stovetop. Only the declared original schematic is available.
- **Shared behavior:** glaze choice, whole quantity 1–4 within stock, unavailable and invalid feedback, displayed item subtotal/shipping/total, first valid add fails, retry retains choices and succeeds with exact variant/quantity/total. The action is explicitly simulated; no payment or delivery promise. Sand × 2 totals USD 90.
- **Conventional:** familiar product image/details columns, option form and complete conditions below. No omitted facts or deliberately oversized image.
- **Adaptation:** compact object sheet groups schematic/dimensions, fit facts, then option/stock/cost and action; care/returns stay visibly connected to the commitment. This uses the [existing commerce plan](../ux-plans/commerce.md) insight that one schematic cannot support a photographic story. Benefit hypothesis: less separation of fit and purchase evidence; tradeoff: less room for immersive imagery. A familiar product page remains equally appropriate with richer product photography.
- **Art direction:** earthy object palette plus a measured schematic/caption treatment; compact specification labels paired with generous object space. Both conditions use the same illustration and truthful caption; no invented glaze texture, handmade claim or testimonial.
- **Checks:** Sand × 2 exact USD 84 + 6 = 90; Ash unavailable; Iron quantity 3 blocked; correction preserves variant; first-add error/retry and exact success. Read care/returns, keyboard choices and focus at 320/390/1440. No full-page-only claim about action visibility.

## Editorial reading — Bench Notes

- **Audience/task:** read a fictional chair-joint field note, distinguish observation from inference and return to a saved section. This is not repair instruction or a safety assessment. Three sections (`observe`, `compare`, `record`) contain complete prose, numbered schematic artifacts and explicit uncertainty; fixture captions explain enlargement and comparison limits.
- **Shared behavior:** ordinary section anchors, focusable target headings, save/replace a section reading place in local page memory, resume and clear; no remote persistence promise. All section text, artifacts and captions remain present. Bookmark absence is a useful entry state; clear restores it.
- **Conventional:** readable single-column article with contents links, figures/captions and a separate field-note summary after the prose.
- **Adaptation:** at wide widths the observation and its numbered artifact/note form an evidence spread; at narrow widths each claim is immediately followed by its own figure/caption/note. The relationship changes from article-then-summary to evidence attached to the relevant assertion. Benefit hypothesis: easier source tracing; tradeoff: a denser rhythm interrupts uninterrupted reading. Conventional remains good for narrative reading.
- **Art direction:** paper-like ground, precise numbered red-brown marks matching the diagrams, serif reading text with system-sans labels. Typography separates prose from records; diagram labels stay legible rather than becoming decorative texture.
- **Checks:** keyboard anchor target focus, each artifact/caption association, long caption wrapping, save Compare → navigate Record → resume Compare → clear at 320/390/1440. Capture actual anchor/resume scroll and focused heading, without reset. No network failure state is invented for local memory.

## Collection exploration — Form Index

- **Audience/task:** distinguish four fictional paper/clay records, filter, inspect metadata and return to the same result. Items differ in type, material, year, dimensions and visual structure; long FI-03 name stresses wrapping. Dates are fixture dates, not historical attribution. No real holdings, availability or commerce.
- **Shared behavior:** search title/material, type All/Print/Object, result count and empty reset, item detail with all metadata/caption, return retaining search/filter/order and triggering-item focus. Every original schematic is labeled as schematic, not a photograph or existing artwork.
- **Conventional:** competent equal-card grid with title/type/year under each thumbnail; ordinary detail page/region and Back to results.
- **Adaptation:** index-like result rows align small image, object identity and discriminating material/dimension facts, while detail pairs a larger object with its record. Narrow rows retain those differentiating facts rather than reducing each entry to a name. Benefit hypothesis: compare unlike objects without opening each one; tradeoff: less image-led discovery and lower thumbnail prominence. Grid remains suitable for visual browsing.
- **Art direction:** archive numbering and object-specific aspect ratios with quiet ruled metadata; maintain the original warm earth/blue/clay differences across results and detail, without turning filters into novelty controls.
- **Checks:** filter Print, search `ink`, open long FI-03, return with query/filter/trigger focus intact; search nonexistent → empty count → reset; detail caption and all metadata at 320/390/1440. Capture natural return viewport rather than assuming results remain visible.
