## Quality verdict and closing the loop

Raw Standard old reports color-contrast violations. Lean reports color-contrast plus definition-list violations: white text on the primary blue button is4.09:1, and a visual separator is an invalid direct child of a definition list. Lean's “Guaranteed Rate” label is also not established by the illustrative fixture, despite its notes claiming grounded copy. These are retained negative findings. Passing task/width checks and lower input usage do not approve this output.

The separate [worked finalization](worked-finalization/README.md) demonstrates the intended focused finishing pass. It darkens the existing blue token, moves the visual divider to a valid definition group and labels the illustrative exchange rate honestly. JavaScript, fixture and original task stay byte-identical. Its own three traces pass63/63 with no reported axe violations at initial/failure/final stages; incomplete ARIA/contrast items remain unassessed. It is an operator correction after feedback, not a new blinded sample or a replacement for the raw paired result. Finalization costs were not separately measured.

The natural checkpoints preserve actual scroll/focus and viewport framing. They do not establish absence of occlusion, good focus order or human comprehension. Optional full-page images have a distinct scope. No manual/assistive-technology study was performed.

## What follows

Use the default single-creator workflow, existing context and relevant summaries; escalate only for a consequential unknown or named blocker. Run available quality checks and reconcile factual copy before completion. A cheaper initial attempt can still require a small correction. Future replication should measure full acceptance-cycle costs and broader tasks; the two-pair probe does not certify Explore or universal UX/resource improvement.
