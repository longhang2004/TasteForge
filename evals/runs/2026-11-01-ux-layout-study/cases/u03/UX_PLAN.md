# Everyday Teapot — UX plan

Primary task: choose an available glaze, set quantity, understand full cost and conditions, then simulate adding to cart. Source of truth: fixture.json and supplied SVG. No external browsing is permitted.

Three structures considered:
1. Side-by-side evidence and purchase panel. First action is glaze selection; price, specifications, delivery and returns remain visible. Edits stay in place. Mobile becomes image → purchase → detailed specifications. Selected for direct comparison without extra navigation.
2. Single editorial sequence: photograph/illustration → craft/specifications → configuration. Better continuous reading, but delays the primary action on mobile.
3. Configurator first with a separate expandable specification area. Fast repeat buying, but hides product-fit information and encourages acting before reading conditions. Rejected.

Region map: studio identity and honest simulation disclosure; breadcrumb; illustration and caption; title/unit price; glaze radios with unavailable Ash; quantity; delivery and returns; shipping-inclusive order breakdown; simulation action and persistent live feedback; material, dimensions and care. Desktop specifications sit below illustration, beside the purchase information. Mobile puts all purchase conditions before the action and specifications after it in normal flow.
