How to read the evidence

Required checks exercise the supplied task contract. Optional diagnostics are reported separately. Axe findings cover the tested states and rules only; zero findings do not establish full accessibility.

This pilot has one sample per condition for each model/prompt. Model randomness is not controlled, the reviewers are AI agents, and the study does not establish a causal or general improvement.
