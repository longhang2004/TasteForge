# Anonymous UI reviews

Two fresh reviewer subagents rated the randomized packet before seeing creator models or generation conditions. Both reported viewing all 32 supplied screenshots and using the recorded browser observations; neither manually operated the interfaces or performed a screen-reader audit.

- [Reviewer r1](reviewer-r1.json): requested model `gpt-6.1-sol`.
- [Reviewer r2](reviewer-r2.json): requested model `gpt-6.1-terra`.
- [Individual ratings CSV](ratings.csv): one row per reviewer, case, and rubric dimension.
- [Original anonymous packet](packet/README.md) and [packet data](packet/packet.json).

Original ratings are preserved without reconciliation. The [report](../report.md) shows disagreements and only descriptive per-dimension means; no aggregate taste score or statistical improvement claim is made. Two model judgments may be correlated and do not replace independent human users.
