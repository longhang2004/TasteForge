Usage: tasteforge inspire [options]

Sample external style references with task-matched structures and fixed review
gates

Options:
  --task <task>     marketing, dashboard, commerce, form, portfolio, editorial,
                    documentation, learning, or booking
  --brief <file>    project-local brief JSON (default: ".tasteforge/brief.json")
  --count <number>  distinct style references (1–20) (default: 3)
  --seed <integer>  unsigned 32-bit seed
  --query <text>    additional task or visual search terms
  --source <id>     restrict the style pool to one catalog source
  -h, --help        display help for command
