D balances its lead with the static preview and keeps plan conditions before the action. A gives the preview and contributors substantial explanation, but its tall mobile form separates the decision from the submit action and the failure from retry. D's inline email error and nearby retry are strongest for correction and recovery in this sample.

All eight scoped check summaries report no failures, including state retention, keyboard retry, invalid-input blocking and stale-success clearing.
