# Independently frozen dimension comparisons

Both initial JSON/Markdown reviews were hashed before the parent opened the identity key. Preference groups run strongest to weaker; equals signs preserve ties. These are separate local dimension judgments, with no aggregate or overall repository ranking. Impeccable here means the constrained native offline path, not its full default online workflow.

[Reviewer 1](reviewer-1.md) inspected 64 actual images; [reviewer 2](reviewer-2.md) inspected 56. Both covered the identical five-image core for all eight variants, plus symmetric invalid/full-page expansion. Reviewer 1 additionally inspected eight wide decision images. Different expansion scope can contribute to disagreement. Full comparative evidence/tradeoffs remain in their [JSON 1](reviewer-1.json) and [JSON 2](reviewer-2.json).
