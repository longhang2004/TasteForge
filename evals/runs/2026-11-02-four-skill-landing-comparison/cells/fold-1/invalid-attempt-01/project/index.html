03 / Before you head out

A few useful details.

How do the editions differ?

Harbor, Ridge and Night have the same listed contents and USD 53 price. This prototype does not provide edition-specific route, color or design differences. Harbor has 4 kits, Ridge has 2, and Night is unavailable.

Are these maps for navigation?

No. The supplied artwork is schematic and fictional. It is not a real navigation map, a photograph, or evidence of print quality.

What happens when I reserve?

The first valid attempt shows a simulated failure. Retry keeps your edition, quantity and email and completes a local simulation. No real reservation, order, payment or email is created.

What about delivery and returns?

Shipping is USD 5 per order. No delivery date is supplied or promised. Unused kits may be returned within 14 days; the buyer pays return postage.
