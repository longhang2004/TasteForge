Before you begin

A few useful
answers.

Can I use my existing Markdown notes?

Yes. The supplied workflow is to paste Markdown change notes, arrange the sections, then prepare an email draft. This landing-page slice illustrates that workflow; it does not implement the editor.

Does the demo connect to Git or send email?

No. No live Git integration or actual sending is implemented or promised here. The setup runs locally and creates no real account, payment or email.

What is included for my team?

Both billing choices cover one workspace with up to 3 editors. Monthly is USD 12 per workspace each month. Annual is USD 120 per workspace billed once yearly. The fictional offer includes a 14-day trial; the demo asks for no card and does not activate it.

What about cancellation and refunds?

No real subscription or payment is processed in this demo. A real cancellation or refund policy beyond this demo has not been supplied.

Demo privacy

Entered values stay in current-page memory. This prototype does not transmit form data, create an account, process payment or send email. No reload persistence, analytics or cookies are used.

Demo terms

Fictional product, workspace offer and change records; local simulation only. No real trial, account, subscription, payment or email is created. No integrations, guarantees or measured outcomes are promised.
