03 · Before you reserve

A few useful details.

What is included?

Two original A3 foldout schematic prints, one paper sleeve and numbered route-note cards. No card count is specified.

How do the editions differ?

All editions have the same listed contents and USD 53 price. Edition specific design, color and route differences are not supplied. The illustrations are schematic, not real navigation maps.

Which editions can I reserve?

Harbor has 4 kits available and Ridge has 2. Night is unavailable. You can choose a whole quantity from 1 to 3, limited by the selected edition’s stock.

What does shipping cost?

Shipping is USD 5 per order. For example, two Harbor kits cost USD 106 plus USD 5 shipping, for USD 111 total. No delivery date is supplied or promised.

Can an unused kit be returned?

Unused kits may be returned within 14 days; the buyer pays return postage. This is a fictional product policy.

What happens when I reserve?

This prototype validates your choices and email locally. The first valid attempt shows a recoverable error. Retry keeps your values and confirms the simulation. No real order, payment or email is sent.
