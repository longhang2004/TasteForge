# Direction decisions

Product mechanism: existing Markdown change notes are arranged as a release-email draft. Maintainers comparing a small-team workspace need workflow and billing clarity, not unprovided performance proof. Their cultural home includes community conferences, contributor meetings and project publications. Category default is a SaaS screenshot hero; its predictable opposite is a simulated terminal. Both are excluded.

Grounded candidates, ordered by resonance before implementation:
1. Contributor meeting agenda: white pages, indigo agenda headings, grouped decision rows; familiar collaboration ritual, but familiar marketing translation.
2. Community project bulletin: navy/white, compact notices and large issue titles; recognizable publishing context with a wide artifact column.
3. Conference program: cobalt field, white bold Manrope display and pale-blue schedule lanes; workflow is a sequence and workspace setup is the working registration line.
4. Library circulation sheet: pale mint, dark green, compact stamps replaced by explicit labels; brings provenance forward but risks reading as an archive.
5. Publication colophon: charcoal, white and plum, strong contributors and credits; offers authorship clarity but delays the task.
6. Community notice wall: lilac ground, plain black type and varied scales; supports distinct project records but risks fragmented reading.
7. Printed workshop handout: cool white, blue tables and practical illustrations; task clarity with less page-scale expression.

Native roll assigned candidate 3. No artistic reroll. seed:7f109aec5a82b3dd.

Challenger fusion and judgment, before borrowing:

- Cyclorama: draft preparation as lighting phases; declined on both axes. Raise: use a decisive transition between opening and artifact regions.
- Sewing pattern: change records as numbered pieces; declined on both axes. Raise: exact labels and conditions next to the commitment.

The experiment explicitly authorizes autonomous direction choice, code-first work, one creator and supplied media. Human confirmation/decision-board round, image comps, separate finisher and separate documenter are overridden for this controlled slice. This is not full native orchestration compliance. No human or independent acceptance is claimed. No network/catalog imagery is fetched.
