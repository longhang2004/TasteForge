# Four-skill landing-page comparison

Predeclared before any creator dispatch. User requested comparing TasteForge, UI/UX Pro Max, Elaya AI Design Skills and Impeccable on2 November2026. [LON-115](https://linear.app/long-hang/issue/LON-115/run-a-pinned-four-skill-landing-page-comparison-with-matched-outputs) tracks this new scope. Earlier frozen studies/runs remain untouched.

## Question and common scope

Compare runnable UI under a **controlled single-creator, code-first skill-layer setup**, using each pinned repository's instructions/resources on the same supplied landing-page task. Assess visual craft, product specificity, task/access quality and observed creation→check→correction resources separately. This tests skill-assisted outputs at this scope, not original authorship, human usability, universal ranking or each repository's full default product workflow.

Two new fictional product briefs, Field Fold and Relay Notes, give eight creator cells. Both are new landing pages: one physical creative kit and one small software offer. This common task avoids drawing a broad app-UI verdict from a landing-specialist entrypoint. No Figma-vs-code comparison or separate redesign/operate/read lane in this pilot. No no-skill control, replication or population-effect claim. These cases differ from teaching studies and previous fresh prompts, while sharing common marketing/decision families.

## Independent comparison

After scoped checks/corrections, build one identical packet for two fresh reviewers who authored none of these outputs. Randomize neutral A/B/C/D labels independently per brief; withhold mapping and creator notes. Supply equal task facts, normal wide/narrow and relevant natural-state image scope, source and checks. Freeze their initial judgments/hashes before unblinding. Blinding is procedural, not immune to inference from styling.

Judge dimensions separately, with ties/unassessed/concerns and image/state evidence:

- **Visual craft:** hierarchy/readability, composition/rhythm, typography/color/media craft, responsive/state coherence. Do not score by a preferred font/palette or number of animations.
- **Product specificity:** supplied facts/artifacts shaping decisions, media/caption/content connection, relationship-aware structure, identity preserved across widths/states without task harm.
- **Task/access quality:** primary action and decision conditions, truthful content, narrow access, validation/retention/recovery, applicable keyboard/focus/contrast/semantics.
- **Resources:** observable creation/check/correction/acceptance coverage and unknowns, separately from UI judgments.

Four-way dimension preferences may have tied groups or no defensible ordering. No aggregate novelty/slop score or forced overall winner. Repo-native detectors/critiques may guide their own creator workflow but are not the common evaluator. Preserve reviewer disagreements and negative results. Do not call sources human-made or layouts unique without evidence.

Publish gallery, per-brief/per-dimension findings, raw versions/failures/corrections, source/resource hashes, resource coverage and limitations. Initial two-brief pilot is reported separately.
