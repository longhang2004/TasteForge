# Two-brief, four-skill comparison

Eight valid runnable UIs have scoped automated acceptance: **276 task assertions, 128 natural state captures and 8 exact-price probes**. This pilot compares pinned skill guidance under the same single-creator, static HTML/CSS/JS, supplied-media/font and offline constraints. Independent visual craft, product specificity and task/access judgments are reported separately in the [frozen comparison](reviews/comparison.md). There is no aggregate repository winner or cost-efficiency verdict.

Use the [interactive gallery](index.html) to switch brief, 1440/390/320px capture and entry/decision/invalid/failure/success state. Every card links its runnable final UI, raw matched checks and creator record. [Acceptance](acceptance.json) identifies the exact checked source bytes. Images preserve actual scroll/focus after task actions; optional wide full-page captures are separately labeled and do not establish simultaneous visibility. Scroll-reveal pages can have unrevealed sections in an entry full-page capture; neither blank capture areas nor document presence establish what a person saw.

## Tasks and treatments

Field Fold is a fictional USD 53 printed-map kit. Edition stock, whole quantities, USD 5 shipping, a supplied return condition and local-only reservation shape the purchase decision. Relay Notes is a fictional release-email drafting workspace with supplied Canal Docs artifacts, exact monthly/annual billing, up to three editors and a local-only trial form. Both require invalid-input handling, retained values after the first valid simulated failure, retry and truthful success. No real order, account, payment or email is created. ## Execution and technical evidence

One fresh creator context per valid cell used the same inherited host/model setting, with no model override; exact resolved backend identity was unavailable. Each received the same brief, facts, assets, fonts, stack, widths and budget. Two creators at most worked concurrently. One focused creator self-correction and one targeted operator pass were allowed; first runnable versions, correction versions and raw evidence remain. There was no artistic reroll or extension to more prompts.

The shared checks exercise CTA access, stock/quantity or billing selection, exact consequences, validation, retained values, simulated failure, keyboard retry, honest success, applicable focus/semantics/contrast, geometry, runtime errors/requests and reduced-motion entry. Accepted passes contain no recorded assertion failures, horizontal overflow or axe violations in their inspected states. Incomplete axe items remain in raw reports. This scope does not certify full accessibility, every animation frame, every input permutation, browser/device coverage, comprehension or human usability.

## Independent review and uncertainty

Two fresh AI reviewers authored none of these UIs. Both received the identical randomized A/B/C/D packet, with labels randomized separately per brief and treatment/creator/resource notes withheld. Initial JSON and Markdown judgments were hashed before unblinding. Normal-color wide/narrow and consequential task states were inspected with image tools; source/checks were used only to clarify named concerns. Each dimension permits tied preference groups and unassessed cases. Procedural blinding cannot prevent inference from style or source conventions. [Frozen review records and exact dimension groups](reviews/index.html) retain evidence, tradeoffs, concerns and disagreements; no aggregate novelty/slop score is computed.

Independent findings are documented in [comparison.md](reviews/comparison.md), with a separate post-freeze operator adjudication. Any remaining concern limits the accepted-demo claim to the stated technical scope. Human/manual usability testing remains deferred.

Both reviewers put TasteForge and constrained Impeccable together in their strongest composition/spacing group for both briefs. In Relay Notes both also place that pair first for coherence across widths/states and preserving the specific decision. Impeccable's readable artifact summaries/full-size local links receive the strongest media-craft group from both. These are local dimension preferences; typography, media ties and recovery order differ between reviewers.

Product specificity is more mixed than a claim of unique identity would suggest. Reviewer 1 ties all four on coherent product direction in each brief; reviewer 2 places Elaya lower and ties the other three. Both give TasteForge and Impeccable strong fact-to-composition placement, while reviewer 2 also includes ProMax in that top group. The familiar hero, editorial and native-form patterns are not treated as defects or uniqueness proof.

The clearest negative TasteForge finding is **Field Fold's invalid-stock cost presentation**: Ridge ×3 is correctly blocked, but a normal-looking USD 164 total remains visible. Both reviewers place TasteForge last on preserving that specific decision without task harm, and place constrained Impeccable first for entry/decision/truthfulness. The parent confirmed the image/source discrepancy. No post-review repair was substituted into the assessed artifact. ProMax's taller narrow forms require more scrolling, offset by useful linked error summaries; Elaya's distant narrow feedback and spacing receive lower preferences on some dimensions, with a disputed media-craft concern. These findings, ties and different inspection expansions stay in the [initial records](reviews/index.html) and [adjudication](reviews/adjudication.md).

## Resources and deviations

The six uninterrupted creator wall spans were approximately 6.4–8.1 minutes, including reading, implementation, self-check/correction and documentation. Accepted root passes took 33.9–50.4 seconds each. Earlier failures, root analysis, setup, review and publication are additional work. Full creator spans for two quota-interrupted cells remain recorded; exact unavailable onset and active creation duration are unknown. Overlapping wall spans are not summed as elapsed effort. See [observed resource coverage](resources.md) and [per-pass resource data](resources.json).

Provider/resolved model, input/output/thinking/cache tokens, billing, complete tool counts, full operator/evaluator time and full accepted-cycle cost are unknown. Static instruction words and initial generation time do not establish end-to-end savings. No monetary, token or efficiency winner is supported. No paid fallback was used.

The original `/tmp` projects vanished across tool invocations, so isolated persistent projects outside the TasteForge Git root replaced them. A ProMax intake-contaminated attempt and an Impeccable network-backed seed attempt were declared invalid before inspecting replacement quality; both remain unscored in [cells](cells/). The valid Impeccable cells used every native call with timeout zero, telemetry disabled and loopback API URL, producing the native degraded offline seed path. This is not a named documented offline mode or a full online-native efficacy test; challenger catalog/quality boards were unavailable. The explicit single-creator/code-first constraint also overrides native comp-first and separate finisher/documenter orchestration. These departures are not scored as UI-quality failures. See [workspace/intake amendment](protocol-amendment-01.md), [offline-native amendment](protocol-amendment-02.md) and [provider interruption amendment](protocol-amendment-03.md). Actual dispatch chronology departed from the intended reverse order; perfect counterbalance is not claimed.

## Practical limits

This is one output per treatment per brief, using two landing-page tasks and a shared constrained host, not a no-skill control, replicated population study or model comparison. The briefs share marketing/decision families with maintained work but do not reuse their artifacts. Supplied schematic media, offline restrictions, one creator and missing full telemetry limit generalization. Full native workflows, image generation, open-web research, Figma, installation/discovery, operate/read tasks, real screen-reader speech, human acceptance and production backends remain unassessed.

The comparison does not modify the TasteForge skill or historical frozen runs. LON-115 owns this bounded evidence; LON-99 source diversity, LON-109 broader full-cycle replication and LON-95 installation/discovery remain separate. No release, npm publication or main merge is included.
